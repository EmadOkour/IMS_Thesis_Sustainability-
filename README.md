---

## Research Question

> How can sustainability obligations arising from the EU Taxonomy and CSRD be systematically translated from organisational policy level to operational implementation across the full hierarchy of CSRD-obligated industrial manufacturing companies — and what governance framework is required to close that translational gap?

---

## Three Contributions

1. **Gap Classification Framework** — process-fixable / transformation-required / regulation-dependent
2. **Cross-BA Governance Architecture** — expert board, unified tiered spec sheet, project lead accountability, Sustainability Council oversight
3. **Taxonomy Compliance Framework** — Zero Harm cascade model (Policy → Building Blocks → Essentials)

---

## Case Study

Siemens Energy FY2025 Annual Report — illustrative case, publicly available document analysis.
